# AUSNUT 2023 data notice

The `lib/ausnut-foods.ts` dataset is derived from **AUSNUT 2023**, produced by Food Standards Australia New Zealand (FSANZ). It was converted from the official Excel files into the app's tuple format, reduced to energy and macronutrients, and scaled to a selected household measure where available.

It is distributed under the [FSANZ Data User Licence Agreement](https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/science-data/monitoringnutrients/afcd/datauserlicenceagreement), based on the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Australia licence. The derived dataset is available under the same licence. This notice does not apply that licence to the rest of the application.

There are limitations associated with food composition databases. Food composition data may represent an average from a particular sample and time. Nutrient composition can vary substantially between batches and brands due to season, processing, ingredient source and calculation methods.

The data is based on Australian foods and may not be appropriate for use in other countries. FSANZ does not sponsor or endorse this application.
